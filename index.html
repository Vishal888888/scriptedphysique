About Vishal

What happens when an IT engineer gets serious about fitness?

Five years ago, I was hitting the gym with no real plan — just hoping something would work. As an engineer, I hate inefficiency. So I studied what actually works, earned my Certificate IV in Fitness and First Aid certification, and started treating fitness like any other problem: step by step, with a clear plan and no wasted time.

Your fitness plan should work with your life, not against it. That's why I focus on your actual schedule, your real constraints, and create something that fits your professional demands.
